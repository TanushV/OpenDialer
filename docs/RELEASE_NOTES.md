# OpenDialer 1.0.6

- **Test call just works.** Test call dials your saved test phone right away, at any hour, even if that number was on an old list. Calling goes back to normal after you save the test call.
- **The Dialer says why no one is callable.** Instead of "No one left to call", it names the reason, such as everyone being outside calling hours.
- **HubSpot is checked end to end.** If your HubSpot has no contact lists yet, Leads says how to make one. A repeated call log finds the first record instead of failing.
- **Calling unblocks itself after time away.** "Call events are catching up after a gap" clears by itself once no call is open.

Updating from 1.0.1, 1.0.3, 1.0.4 or 1.0.5 keeps your workspace, numbers, script and saved credentials.
Updating from 0.9.x or 0.10.x keeps your workspace and saved credentials. Updating from 1.0.0 also preserves your workspace, numbers, playbook and calling settings. macOS may ask for microphone access again.
Versions 0.2 and 0.3 start a fresh workspace and old data stays untouched in its previous folder.
Updating from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1 may require you to re-enter keys and re-import setup or login files.
