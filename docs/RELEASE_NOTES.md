# OpenDialer 1.0.3

- **HubSpot lists.** Add a HubSpot Service Key in Settings, choose who you call for, and sync a HubSpot list from Leads. Numbers are checked before they are dialed, opt-outs flow back, and each call is logged in HubSpot.
- **Why now.** Each prospect brief shows the trigger that makes this a good week to call, and the script can carry a trigger list.
- **Any CSV layout.** Importing a list shows how its columns were read so you can fix the mapping before anything is saved.
- **Calls stay steady.** Pausing no longer pauses itself again in a loop, and a busy carrier line waits a minute and resumes instead of failing.
- **No false block after a hang-up.** When the person you called hangs up, calling no longer stops with a transcription error.
- **Stay ready between calls.** The audio connection renews itself after each call, so you no longer press Go ready again.
- **See who hung up.** After a call ends, the Dialer shows "They hung up" or "You ended the call" with the length of the call.
- **Nothing covers your next step.** Save outcome and Next call stay clear of the call dock and notices, even in a small window, and a screen error shows a Reload button instead of a blank window.
- **Windows is back.** OpenDialer installs on Windows again, with the same calling, keep-awake and updates as the Mac.

Updating from 1.0.1 keeps your workspace, numbers, script and saved credentials.
Updating from 0.9.x or 0.10.x keeps your workspace and saved credentials. Updating from 1.0.0 also preserves your workspace, numbers, playbook and calling settings. macOS may ask for microphone access again.
Versions 0.2 and 0.3 start a fresh workspace and old data stays untouched in its previous folder.
Updating from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1 may require you to re-enter keys and re-import setup or login files.
