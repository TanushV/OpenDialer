# OpenDialer 1.0.4

- **Pause always works.** A click on Pause right after Start is no longer lost, so the Dialer never dials the next lead after you paused.
- **A steadier screen.** Background work such as lead research no longer redraws the whole app many times a second.
- **Review shows what needs you.** Review opens with a short list of issues, each with the one thing to do, then follow-ups and past calls.
- **Learning starts sooner.** Best hours show from 3 calls, and a learned model is tried from 40 calls instead of 100. It is used only once it beats the starting estimates.
- **Cleaner Leads and Numbers.** Leads has one toolbar row and shows bulk actions only once you select a lead. Numbers shows your numbers first.
- **HubSpot lists.** Add a HubSpot Service Key in Settings, choose who you call for, and sync a HubSpot list from Leads.
- **Windows is back.** OpenDialer installs on Windows again, with the same calling and updates as the Mac.

Updating from 1.0.1 or 1.0.3 keeps your workspace, numbers, script and saved credentials.
Updating from 0.9.x or 0.10.x keeps your workspace and saved credentials. Updating from 1.0.0 also preserves your workspace, numbers, playbook and calling settings. macOS may ask for microphone access again.
Versions 0.2 and 0.3 start a fresh workspace and old data stays untouched in its previous folder.
Updating from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1 may require you to re-enter keys and re-import setup or login files.
