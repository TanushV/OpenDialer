# OpenDialer 1.0.7

- **Calling repairs itself.** If something that should clear by itself does not, the app repairs it. If that fails, the Dialer shows a Reconnect button instead of waiting forever.
- **See what Start will do.** While paused, the Dialer shows how many leads can be called right now, or the plain reason none can, checked every minute.
- **Leads and Lists on one page.** Your lists sit above your leads, and changing a list updates both.
- **A clear Script page.** It says whether your changes are saved, offers Discard, and folds the usage tools away.
- **Test call just works.** Test call dials your saved test phone right away, at any hour.

Updating from 1.0.1, 1.0.3, 1.0.4, 1.0.5 or 1.0.6 keeps your workspace, numbers, script and saved credentials.
Updating from 0.9.x or 0.10.x keeps your workspace and saved credentials. Updating from 1.0.0 also preserves your workspace, numbers, playbook and calling settings. macOS may ask for microphone access again.
Versions 0.2 and 0.3 start a fresh workspace and old data stays untouched in its previous folder.
Updating from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1 may require you to re-enter keys and re-import setup or login files.
