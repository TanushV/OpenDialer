# OpenDialer 1.0.8

- **A summary after every call.** When a call ends, one AI summary of the whole conversation appears in the wrap-up with a suggested outcome and next step, and goes to the HubSpot call record. It uses your OpenRouter key on a no-retention route.
- **Transcripts kept 30 days.** Call transcripts stay on this computer for 30 days, then are deleted. Delete one sooner from Review. An opt-out deletes that call's transcript.
- **Nothing jumps on Leads.** The lists table sits below your leads, so the page no longer shifts as it loads.
- **Calling repairs itself.** If something that should clear by itself does not, the app repairs it, or shows Reconnect.

Updating from 1.0.1, 1.0.3, 1.0.4, 1.0.5, 1.0.6 or 1.0.7 keeps your workspace, numbers, script and saved credentials.
Updating from 0.9.x or 0.10.x keeps your workspace and saved credentials. Updating from 1.0.0 also preserves your workspace, numbers, playbook and calling settings. macOS may ask for microphone access again.
Versions 0.2 and 0.3 start a fresh workspace and old data stays untouched in its previous folder.
Updating from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1 may require you to re-enter keys and re-import setup or login files.
