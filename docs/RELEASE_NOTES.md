# OpenDialer 1.0.9

- **A simpler live helper.** While the prospect talks, one short line you could say or ask next; press F1 or type a question for a private answer. Tips are never saved. It needs your OpenRouter key and says so when it is missing.
- **Review shows the summary.** Each call in Review shows its outcome, the after-call summary and next step, your own note and the transcript. A summary that could not be written has Retry.
- **The next call remembers.** The next script and brief for a lead read the last call's summary, next step and your note.
- **Every key answers at once.** Hang up, Skip, Stay and the outcome keys show on screen the moment you press them, and an outcome pressed while the call is still ending is saved once it ends instead of being lost.
- **Settings is one list.** Each row holds its own control: the ChatGPT login, the call connection and calling hours are set right in their rows.
- **Only phone facts save themselves.** No answer, busy and voicemail are saved for you; what a person said is a suggestion you confirm with Enter.

Updating from 1.0.1, 1.0.3, 1.0.4, 1.0.5, 1.0.6, 1.0.7 or 1.0.8 keeps your workspace, numbers, script and saved credentials.
Updating from 0.9.x or 0.10.x keeps your workspace and saved credentials. Updating from 1.0.0 also preserves your workspace, numbers, playbook and calling settings. macOS may ask for microphone access again.
Versions 0.2 and 0.3 start a fresh workspace and old data stays untouched in its previous folder.
Updating from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1 may require you to re-enter keys and re-import setup or login files.
