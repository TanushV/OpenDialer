# OpenDialer 0.5.0

- Plain, readable scripts on the calling screen. Upload Markdown or text in Intelligence, preview automatically separated sections, and retain version history and controlled variations.
- Space or Next call asks the scheduler for the next eligible prospect. Second attempts remain scheduler-selected and respect retry and campaign limits. Reloading restores the current reserved lead and its script.
- Optional next call after ended voicemail, with a five-second cancel window. Pause after call takes precedence; only one prospect is active.
- Confirmed carrier results and supported, independently reviewed AI dispositions save automatically. Human corrections always win. Bookings, ambiguous results and callback times still need review.
- Simulated workspace entry and call controls are hidden in release builds. Practice focuses on observed weak patterns, including supported statistical findings.
- Intelligence opens on the script, with memory, statistical results and product facts in expandable panels. Number workload adjusts to observed delivery within reviewed limits. Planning saves preserve edits made while an earlier save is pending.
- Research and post-call providers submit named tool calls for application validation. Live helper presentation retains streaming. AI-saved outcomes are not observed training labels until confirmed by a person.

Updates from 0.4.x retain the existing workspace. Updating from 0.2 or 0.3 starts a fresh workspace; old data stays untouched in its previous folder and is not migrated.

Updater signatures verify artifact integrity. Apple notarization, Windows publisher signing, clean-machine installation, live carrier/audio/inference behavior and campaign lift remain separate gates.
