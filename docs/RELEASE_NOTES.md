# OpenDialer 1.0.0

- **Your Markdown playbook.** Use your own script with pitch alternatives, objection branches and separate rep directions. Agite keeps its saved workspace playbook.
- **Fresh scripts for each lead.** Meaningful template edits regenerate scripts from current verified facts and call history. Choose a list, reuse research or refresh it, and measure a sample before rewriting the rest.
- **Visible model usage.** See reported input, output, cached and reasoning tokens by master version, list, lead and work type. Failed requests retain their reported consumption; missing counters remain unknown.
- **Lists and local calling hours.** Choose the active dialing pool, manage list membership and stop new outbound calls at 5 p.m. in the lead's local time. Your existing numbers and callback setup remain in place.

Updating from 0.9.x or 0.10.x keeps your workspace and saved credentials. This is the verified 0.10.2 application released as 1.0.0, with no calling-policy or audio-path change. macOS may ask for microphone access again.
Versions 0.2 and 0.3 start a fresh workspace and old data stays untouched in its previous folder.
Updating from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1 may require you to re-enter keys and re-import setup or login files.
