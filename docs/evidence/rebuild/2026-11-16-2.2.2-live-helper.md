# OpenDialer 2.2.2 live helper timing

The installed 2.2.1 test showed tips on a live call but lacked timestamps for the last word and the visible reply. This check uses labeled synthetic speech and audio replay to isolate those delays.

The existing Telnyx Soniox WebSocket received four complete phrases and three versions of a sentence with an inserted pause. Across two passes all 14 samples produced a final transcript after the last word. The four standalone phrases finalized in 327–463 ms. One short yes produced a final without an interim in both passes. When present, the last interim for the standalone phrases arrived 99–266 ms after the last word. In the pass that recorded flags, all final transcripts carried `speech_final`. Telnyx split at inserted pauses of 440 ms and 700 ms in both passes and did not split at 260 ms. Two false `speech_final` signals were therefore recorded in the flagged pass. No separate `utterance_end` event was observed.

The WebKit Dialer replay used the normal local worker and UI. The paired comparison presented five identical audio patterns and kept model response delays fixed across versions. Each version made five model requests. The stopwatch started when the final speech packet was submitted and stopped when the first reply appeared in the Dialer.

| Replay | First tip range | Median first tip | Model replies shown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2.2.1, fixed model delays | 891–1,224 ms | 1,186 ms | 3 of 5 |
| 2.2.2, fixed model delays | 678–804 ms | 693 ms | 3 of 5 |
| 2.2.2, configured OpenRouter model with fictional turns | 685–794 ms | 724 ms | 4 of 5, at 863–1,101 ms |

In the live-model replay, the first scenario correctly got 60 hours from 20 sites at 3 hours each. The model added an approximate calendar conversion without a stated work schedule. The compliance and onboarding questions got separate replies. One request timed out and the local reply stayed visible. These tests used no carrier phone call, recordings or customer transcripts.

A combined replay sent five synthetic utterances through the real Telnyx transcription stream, the configured OpenRouter model, and the WebKit Dialer. All five turns produced visible tips 596–753 ms after the last speech packet. All five displayed model wording at 753–957 ms. The utterance with a 440 ms pause produced one request, and the estimate reply calculated 60 hours from the spoken inputs. In another turn the model offered an unsupported rollout timeline.
